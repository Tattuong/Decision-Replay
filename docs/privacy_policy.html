Privacy Policy – Decision Replay

Last updated: August 2026

Decision Replay is a notes app for text, checklists, photos and voice memos. Your notes, labels, coins balance and shop preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal data to any server.

Data stored on device

  • Your notes, checklists, photos and voice memo files
  • Labels, colors and pinned notes
  • Coins balance and shop purchases (local record)
  • App preferences: theme, background and card skin

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – for in-app purchases (handled entirely by Google)
  • Remote config – we fetch https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N226.json to check In-App Purchase availability (no personal data is sent in this request)

Permissions

  • Internet – in-app purchases and remote config check
  • Camera – attach photos to notes (optional)
  • Microphone – record voice memos (optional)
  • Photo library – attach images to notes (optional)

Children's privacy

Decision Replay does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal data of any kind.
